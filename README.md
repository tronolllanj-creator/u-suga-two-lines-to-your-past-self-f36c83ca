A program that writes two lines to its past self — a warning about one specific moment and why it mattered. The shipped two lines are:

"Answer the phone when the hospital calls about your mother at four in the morning.
Letting that ring go to voicemail robbed you of the only goodbye you were ever owed."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
